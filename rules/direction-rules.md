# Direction rules for every Nuke In Action video (learned from Karthik's references)

## Story & script
- Write a direction sheet first: look, key moment per shot, motion, framing, palette (3–5 colours), avoid-list.
- Hook (first line AND first frames) must open a curiosity gap and cause an emotion in under a second.
- Every moment must feel like the best thing the viewer could be watching — no dull stretches.
- Make it to be *sent*: one fact that makes the viewer feel smart; CTA "Send this to …".
- Humour + curiosity. Specific facts and numbers. Topics never repeat (topics-done.md).
- Titles come from the most-viewed existing videos on the topic, not invented.

## Look
- Blocky brick-game characters (3D block avatars, blocky animals), detailed large-production sets with depth,
  varied cinematic angles, not flat sets.
- One key still per shot first; small props drawn bigger than instinct; one clear subject per frame.
- Numbers and big words land on objects in the scene (signs, tags, boards, screens), not floating cards.
- Text-behind-subject: the hero's name stands huge behind them, partly covered.
- A diagram that builds with the words (labels type, arrows draw, a moving-marker timeline/counter).
- A repeated/clone grid on multiplier lines ("194 men", "the rats came").
- Handwriting when a line mentions writing; picture-in-picture over a darkened grid for quoted lines.

## Motion & camera
- Camera moves only on story events: gentle push/pan/pull 0.6–1.5 s ease-in-out for transitions; fast push ~0.28 s only
  for reveals/key numbers, then pull out or pan; never the same move every shot. No push-ins that end on a face/eyes.
- Three motion layers per shot: main action + secondary + 2–4 ambient loops. Anticipation → release → settle; no linear
  motion; entrances ease-out, exits ease-in. No jitter/vibration.
- Jokes land on a cut to a reaction close-up with a beat of silence.
- A cut or beat change about every 2–3 s; transitions at sentence breaks: whip/spin, one glitch-slice on a key word.
- Anti-slideshow check before delivery (no still stretch > ~1 s except the joke beat).

## Edit
- Persistent one-line context header on top; word-by-word captions, 1–3 word chunks, the stakes word ~1.45× bigger.
- Impact card (black, big red words) on the payoff line; CTA pill under the captions at the end.
- Perfect loop: the last frame flows into the first. Never end on a branding card.

## Audio
- English narration, Qwen3-TTS speaker Aiden: smooth, warm, subtle, relaxed — no breathiness or gasps; no speed-up.
- NO background music. NO background voices/crowd chatter.
- Effects and beds quiet, well under the voice (mix: effects at voice − 17 LU, ducked under speech).
